=========================================================
PROJECT OBJECTIVE
=========================================================

This project is for an AI Hackathon.

The objective is to create an autonomous AI technology persona that operates independently after a single initialization.

Unlike traditional AI tools that wait for prompts, SENTINEL AI behaves like an autonomous AI technology analyst.

After initialization it continuously:

• discovers live AI and technology topics
• evaluates editorial value
• rejects low-quality topics
• remembers previous publications
• avoids duplicate ideas
• writes in a consistent personality
• automatically publishes over time
• explains WHY every post was published

The application must clearly demonstrate autonomous reasoning rather than simple AI content generation.
